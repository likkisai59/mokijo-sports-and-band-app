1. PLAYER / USER (independent)

Why this role exists: book courts, join pickup games, enroll with trainers, and review venues — without joining a club.

Simple story: “I just want to play today.” They are a customer of the marketplace, not staff of a club.

Pin-to-pin

PinWhat they doWhyWhat happens next
1Open Mokijo → Register as Player (name, email, phone, password)Need an account that is not a club, so they can pay and keep bookings.Account created. Go to Player login. No Super Admin wait.
2Login User → User DashboardProve identity so payments and bookings attach to them.Home: featured venues, Book / Host / Join / Trainings.
3Discover venues, pick date + slotsFind a court at the right time and price.Ready to hold a slot before someone else takes it.
4Hold slot (AVAILABLE → HELD)Lock the time for a few minutes while they pay. Stops double booking.Booking = pending_payment (or pending_approval if owner must say yes).
5Pay on Razorpay (or wait for owner approve first)Venue only confirms after money (or after owner trust check).Success → CONFIRMED, slots BOOKED, payment PAID. Fail → slot released.
6See My BookingsKnow if they can show up, or if still waiting/rejected.Statuses: pending_approval → confirmed / rejected / cancelled.
7Optional: cancelPlans change; slot should go back to others.Slots freed. If paid → refund path.
8Review the venueHelp next players trust good facilities and avoid bad ones.Rating on venue updates.
9Host a pickup game (sport, spots, price, public/private)Need more players to fill a booked slot or social match.Game = open. Host is auto confirmed.

2. CLUB ADMIN

Why this role exists: operate one sports club — people, calendar, money, matches — as a gated tenant on Mokijo.

Simple story: “I run this club. I need roster, events, fees, and live scores in one place.” Super Admin must approve the club first so fake clubs cannot collect money.

8Create event, invite all/groups/guests, optional feePractice, match day, meeting — people must know and RSVP.Invitees: pending → accepted / declined / maybe / waitlisted.
9Mark attendance (present / absent / late)Official record for coaches and parents. Members cannot mark themselves (prevents cheating).Attendance saved on the event.
10Create club course / academySeason program inside the club (different from independent Trainer).Members enroll; seats/fees tracked.

They cannot: approve other clubs or venue-owner accounts (Super Admin); verify venue GPS/docs queue (Mukijo Admin); set venue slots or take venue payouts; use the independent Player dashboard with this login.

3. CLUB MEMBER (Player / Parent / Coach / Referee)

Why this role exists: belong to ONE club after admin approval — RSVP, attend, pay/donate, take club programs. This is not the independent Player.

Simple story: “I (or my child) am on this club’s team.” Sub-roles change the home screen: athlete, parent, squad coach, or referee — same login type.

4dIf Referee: live matches only (events/members blocked)Referee should score, not manage parents or fees.Match screens only on mobile nav.

They cannot: create groups, approve applicants, create/delete campaigns, open admin payments page (members are redirected), run Super/Mukijo admin, own venues, or use Trainer dashboard. Club Coach ≠ independent Trainer.

4. TRAINER (independent coach)

Why this role exists: sell training sessions to anyone on Mokijo (players and club people) — not as club staff.

Simple story: “I am a coach with my own batches.” No Super Admin wait, because they are a service provider, not a club taking memberships.
PinWhat they doWhyWhat happens next
1Register TrainerNeed a coach identity separate from club Coach member.Can login immediately (no approval queue).
2Login → Trainer Dashboard overviewSee how many trainings are live.Open create / list / registrations.
3Create training (title, dates, fee, capacity, location)Publish a batch people can find and pay for.Listed open/draft. Visible on Player Discover + club My Trainings.
4Edit / delete / reschedule (reason required, e.g. rain)Weather and ground issues are real; enrollees must be told why.Players see reschedule_reason on their training list.
5Open Registrations per courseKnow who is coming and who paid (user / club_admin / member / guest).Read-only list: paid / unpaid / waived. (This UI does not approve/reject.)
They cannot: approve club members, own venues, run Super/Mukijo admin, or use club-coach login for this dashboard. Club Coach uses member login.

5. VENUE OWNER

Why this role exists: list real courts, get them verified, sell time slots, approve bookings when needed, and receive payouts.

Simple story: “I own the ground.” Two gates exist: Super Admin must approve the account; Mukijo Admin verifies each venue listing (GPS/docs) so players do not pay fake facilities.
PinWhat they doWhyWhat happens next
1Register owner + venuesNeed a business account that can receive booking money.Owner = PENDING_APPROVAL. Venues = DRAFT. Login blocked.
2Wait Super Admin account approvalStop fake owners listing courts and taking Razorpay.APPROVED → can login-venue. (Approve may also mark venues VERIFIED as a shortcut.)
3Login → overview (bookings, revenue, occupancy)Daily ops: is the ground filling, is money coming.Open my-venues, slots, bookings, payouts.
4Edit venue profile (sports, hours, amenities)Players book from this listing. Wrong hours = angry customers.Listing updated. Still need verification if DRAFT.
5Submit verification wizard: contact → GPS → documentsProve the place is real and reachable.DRAFT → PENDING_VERIFICATION → Mukijo Admin queue.
6Track verification; resubmit if MORE_INFO_REQUIREDAdmin asked for a clearer photo/GPS — fix it to go live.VERIFIED → bookable. REJECTED / SUSPENDED → cannot take new bookings.
7Create / block slotsInventory of sellable time. Block = maintenance or private use.available / held / booked / blocked.
8Approve or reject pending_approval bookingsSome venues want a human yes before a stranger arrives (trust, lighting, membership rules).Approve → player can pay/confirm. Reject → slots released.
9Watch payouts (revenue, platform fee, transferred)That is why they listed the venue — get paid after confirmed bookings.processing → transferred.
10Optional: create/manage matches at the facilityHost a fixture on their ground with live score.Same match lifecycle as club: scheduled → live → completed.
They cannot: approve their own account or other owners’ venues; run club member intake; create player pickup games as this role; publish trainer catalogs.

6. SUPER ADMIN (platform gatekeeper)

Why this role exists: decide WHO may operate on Mokijo — club admins and venue-owner accounts. Not day-to-day coaching or scoring.

Simple story: “I turn the key for businesses.” If Super Admin is skipped, anyone could open a club or venue and collect money.
PinWhat they doWhyWhat happens next
1Login at Super Admin (separate from club/player)This power must not sit on a normal dashboard.Overview: counts of clubs pending/approved/members.
2Open Pending Approvals → ClubsRead club profile before they can charge members.Approve → club can login. Reject + reason → still locked.
3Open Pending → Venue ownersSame gate for people who will take court payments.Approve → owner can login-venue. Approve can also set their venues VERIFIED (shortcut).
4Browse approved / all clubs (filter pending/approved/rejected)Directory and audit: who is live, who was refused.No change unless they act on a row.
They cannot (as this role): run the document/GPS verification queue (that is Mukijo Admin); approve club members (Club Admin does that); approve trainers (trainers have no this gate); book courts or score matches as Super Admin.

7. MUKIJO ADMIN (venue quality / trust)

Why this role exists: verify EACH venue listing (GPS, photos, documents) so players only pay for real grounds. Different from Super Admin account approval.

Simple story: Super Admin says “this business may exist.” Mukijo Admin says “this court is real, here, and safe to book.”
PinWhat they doWhyWhat happens next
1Login Admin (not on the public home hub)QA staff should not be mixed with club registration.Land on venue-verification queue.
2Filter queue: PENDING / UNDER_REVIEW / VERIFIED / MORE_INFO / SUSPENDEDWork the inbox like a checklist, not miss a new listing.Open one venue.
3Start reviewMark “someone is looking” so two admins do not clash.PENDING_VERIFICATION → UNDER_REVIEW.
4Check GPS, photos, docs, contactFake pin or stolen photos = players arrive at empty land.Decision pins below.
5aApproveListing is trustworthy.VERIFIED → players can book. Owner notified.
5bRequest more infoAlmost OK but photo/GPS unclear — don’t reject forever.MORE_INFO_REQUIRED → owner resubmits.
5cRejectNot a real / allowed facility.REJECTED. Not bookable.
5dSuspend laterComplaints, safety, or expired docs after it was live.SUSPENDED → no new bookings.
They cannot: approve club/venue-owner accounts (Super Admin page); submit docs as the owner. Note: Super Admin “approve owner” can skip this queue by setting venues VERIFIED immediately — that is a platform shortcut, not Mukijo Admin’s job.

How the 7 roles fit together (why all of them)

If any role is missing, a part of trust or money breaks.

If we remove…What breaks (why they exist)
PlayerNobody books courts or joins games as a casual customer.
Club AdminNo official club roster, events, fundraising, or live club matches.
Club MemberClub has no athletes/parents to RSVP and pay dues.
TrainerNo independent coaching marketplace (only club-internal coaches).
Venue OwnerNo real inventory of slots; nowhere to play or pay for a ground.
Super AdminAnyone could open a fake club/venue and collect Razorpay.
Mukijo AdminA fake GPS pin could still get booked even after the owner account is approved.

Two different “players”: independent User Dashboard vs club Member after login-member. Do not mix them. Fundraising donate is on the club dashboard.
